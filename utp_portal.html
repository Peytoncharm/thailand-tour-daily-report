How the revenue share is applied

The rule applied to every journey above, published so the calculation can be checked.

Chargeable test — all conditions must be true A journey carries the 10% revenue share when it starts or ends at U-Tapao International Airport, the customer was introduced by an airline or travel agent partner of the airport — recorded in writing at the time of introduction — and the journey has been completed and paid for.

Carries the 10%

  • Bookings from airline partners introduced by the airport
  • Bookings from travel agent partners introduced by the airport
  • Both directions — to and from the airport

Shown, but no share due

  • Terminal booth walk-ups — the booth rent already pays for this traffic
  • Operator website — passengers who found and booked the operator directly
  • LINE and telephone — direct enquiries to the operator

Information deliberately not shown

The portal covers U-Tapao journeys only. It carries no passenger names or contact details, no driver identities, no supplier or driver costs, no margin, and no records from the operator's other routes or business units.

The page refreshes through the day from completed, paid bookings. The month's figure is confirmed on the 1st and becomes the amount payable; the settled figure, not the running total, is what falls due.
